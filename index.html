Self-driving city plain JavaScript · neuroevolution

What am I looking at?

A road network of junctions and roads. Every road is split into directed lanes, and each car follows a lane to a destination, so routing is solved for you — what the cars have to learn is driving.

Each car is a small neural network. It sees a fan of seven range-finder rays plus a few lane signals, and it outputs steering and throttle. There is no coding and no training data: the population is scored on how far it drives without leaving the road, and the winners reproduce. Watch the blue line in the chart climb.

Press E to build your own roads — drag on the map, and the cars keep learning on whatever you draw.
